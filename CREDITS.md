# Credits and licences

Every third-party asset, font, sound and library used by SémioGarde, with its licence (README §17).

## 3D models and textures

No third-party models or textures are bundled yet. All buildings, characters, vehicles, trees and props are built in code from primitive shapes (`src/three/primitives/`). The zellige floor pattern is drawn procedurally on a canvas. Real GLB models arrive in Phase 4 and will be listed here, each with its author and licence.
